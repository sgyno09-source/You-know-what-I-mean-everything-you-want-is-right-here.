### 10 | Peephole Fisheye Girlfriend Expression Series
A consistent photorealistic doorway series using one clearly adult woman, one fixed cream camisole + gray lounge-pants outfit, one apartment-door setting, and one 180-degree circular peephole/fisheye lens. Only the expression, gaze direction, and hand gesture change.

Built-in Looks: Playful Wink, Caught by Surprise, Holding Back a Laugh, Hair-Twirl Smirk, Shy Wish, Yay Celebration, and Kissy Pout. The Skill emphasizes circular black vignette, subtle blue-purple chromatic aberration, retro CCD / early-2000s digital-camera texture, candid natural daylight, and minimal handwritten heart doodles.
